# VilouraAI — 60-Second Phone & Walk-In Script

Read it out loud before you call. Sixty seconds, then stop talking and listen. Never claim a result, a review, or a client we don't have.

## Phone (60 seconds)

> "Hi, I'm Adharsh — I go by Jim — with VilouraAI, here in Lewisville. Quick one: when you're with a customer and the phone rings, who picks up? [Pause.]
>
> I ask because most callers don't leave a voicemail — they call the next business. I do two things for shops like yours. One, an AI receptionist that answers every call, books the appointment, and texts you a summary — $149 a month, first 14 days free. Two, a new 5-page website, live in 7 days, for $497 one-time.
>
> Can I book you a 15-minute call this week to show you what a call sounds like — or is there a better time?"

If yes: book on Calendly (https://calendly.com/vilouraai) and confirm their phone and email.
If no: "No problem — thanks for your time. If the phones ever get ahead of you, that's what we fix."

## Walk-in (60 seconds)

> "Hi, I'm Jim with VilouraAI, just down the road in Lewisville. I help local businesses stop losing customers to missed calls and broken websites. Two quick things: an AI receptionist that answers every call and books the appointment — $149 a month, first 14 days free — and a new website, live in 7 days, for $497.
>
> Can I leave you this one-pager [offer sheet] and book a 15-minute call this week? Or is there a better time to come back?"

Leave the offer sheet. Do not pitch past the door — one page, one next step.

## If they ask "what does it sound like?"

Play or read the sample call on https://vilouraai.com/demo#receptionist — and say plainly it is a written sample, not a recording of a real customer.

## If they ask "who else uses it?"

"We're new — our first 10 clients get the founding price. I don't have past clients to point to yet, which is why the first 14 days of the receptionist are free."
