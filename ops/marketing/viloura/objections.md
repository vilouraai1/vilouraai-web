# VilouraAI — Top 8 Objections

Short answers. Never invent a result, a review, or a client we don't have. If you don't know, say so and offer to check.

## 1. "I already have a website."

"Great — is it bringing you bookings? If it's more than a few years old, has no booking button, or doesn't show up on Google, that's what the $497 rebuild fixes. Want me to take a 2-minute look and tell you honestly whether it's fine as is?"

## 2. "I don't want a robot answering my phone."

"Fair — nobody wants a robot menu. It speaks in a natural voice, introduces itself by name, and books the appointment. And if it can't answer something, it doesn't guess — it takes a message and texts you. You can hear a sample call at vilouraai.com/demo#receptionist, and the first 14 days are free."

## 3. "My customers want to talk to a person."

"They do — and right now, when you're busy, they talk to no one. The receptionist is the backup that answers when you can't. Anything it can't handle comes straight to you as a text summary."

## 4. "$497 / $149 is too much."

"The website is one-time, and it's yours. The receptionist is $149 a month, cancel anytime — one booked appointment a month usually covers it. And the first 14 days are free, so you can hear it work on real calls before you pay anything."

## 5. "I need to think about it."

"Of course. What's the main thing to think through — the price, or whether the AI will sound right? If it's the sound, hear the sample call on the demo page and tell me what you think. I'll check back next week."

## 6. "Can I just get the website / just the receptionist?"

"Yes — they're separate. Website Launch is $497 on its own; the receptionist is $149 a month. The bundle is $597 because the pieces come to $665 separately — and $497 for the first 10 founding clients."

## 7. "What happens to my phone number?"

"You keep it. The receptionist answers on your existing business number. Your customers call the same number they always have."

## 8. "What do you do with my customers' data?"

"We use your business details only to run your site and answer your calls. We don't sell your data or your customers' data, and we don't share it with anyone except the tools needed to run the service. Happy to walk through that on the call before you sign up."
