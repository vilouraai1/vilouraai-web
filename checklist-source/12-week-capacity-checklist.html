Read the pattern, then act

Over 90% full, 2+ weeks out — crunch week coming. Open extra hours or add staff now. Start a waitlist instead of turning people away. Hold full price.

Under 60% full, 2 weeks out — quiet week coming. This is the only week an offer earns its keep. Run it here, not on a week that would have filled anyway.

Three weeks climbing in a row — demand is building. Check staffing and supplies before the crunch lands, not during it.

One wild week that breaks the pattern — ask why. Holiday, school break, local event? Note it. Next year, the same week will do it again.

Cancellations over 15% in a week — confirm bookings. A same-day reminder call or text usually pulls it back under 10%.

End of the 12 weeks

Look back across all 12 rows and answer three questions: Which weeks turned people away? Which weeks sat half-empty? What one change — hours, staffing, pricing — would have fixed both? That answer is your plan for the next 12 weeks. Repeat the sheet.
